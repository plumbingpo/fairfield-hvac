Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast, throughout Fairfield and the surrounding area.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Fairfield HVAC program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation before peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
